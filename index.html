A Dedicated Delivery

For Neelam

Hey, Neelam. I don't know if you'll believe this, and maybe you don't have to believe it right away. But I hope, someday, you do. You are worth it. You don't have to earn someone's kindness. You don't have to become someone better, prettier, stronger, or more perfect before you deserve to be thought of. Sometimes, someone thinks of you simply because you crossed their mind and made them want to do something kind for you. And sometimes, someone sees something in you that you have forgotten to see in yourself. So please don't look at a thoughtful gesture and wonder, "Am I worth receiving this?" You are. Not because you've done something to deserve it. Not because you owe anything in return. Not because you have to give the same feeling back. Just because you're you. And if somewhere inside you there's a little voice telling you that you're not someone worth waiting for, worth caring for, worth choosing, or worth being loved deeply— I hope you learn to be a little kinder to that girl. Because she deserves more kindness from herself than she probably realizes. Maybe some things aren't meant to be measured by whether we think we're worthy of them. Maybe sometimes we simply let someone be kind to us. So keep this little reminder somewhere in your heart: You don't have to prove that you're worth choosing. You already are. — Saksham ❤️
